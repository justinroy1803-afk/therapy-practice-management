# Therapy Practice Management Resources

Managing a therapy practice requires more than providing excellent patient care. Therapists also need organized systems for scheduling appointments, maintaining clinical documentation, tracking patient progress, and managing administrative responsibilities.

## What Is Therapy Practice Management Software?

Therapy practice management software helps healthcare professionals organize their daily clinical and administrative workflows.

Some useful capabilities include:

- Appointment scheduling and calendar management
- Clinical documentation and therapy notes
- Patient progress tracking
- Billing and invoicing
- Appointment reminders
- Secure telehealth services

## Software Resources for Therapy Professionals

Occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, psychologists, and pediatric therapy providers can benefit from digital tools that support their professional workflows.

Choosing suitable software can help practices organize information and manage routine administrative tasks.
